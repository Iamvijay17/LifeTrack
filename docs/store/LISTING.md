## Full description
LifeTrack is one calm place for the things you are trying to get done and the habits that get you there.

• Planner & tasks: plan your day, repeat tasks, and see your progress.
• Habits: check in with one tap; daily, weekly or custom schedules with real streaks and a heatmap.
• Career & learning: topics, goals and study sessions with a built-in timer.
• Job search: track applications, statuses and interviews.
• Fitness & health: workouts, body measurements, sleep, water and steps.
• Finance: expenses, income, budgets and savings goals.
• Focus timer, notes & journal, goals with milestones, and analytics that bring it together.
• Reminders you control: optional push notifications, set in your own time zone.

Your data syncs securely across your phone and the web. No ads, no tracking SDKs, and you can export your data or delete your account at any time.
