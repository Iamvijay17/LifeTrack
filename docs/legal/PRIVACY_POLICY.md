LifeTrack is a personal life-tracking app (planner, habits, learning, jobs, fitness, health, finance, notes, goals, reminders). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

## What we collect
**Account.** Your email address, a password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider; we never see it), and the display name you choose.

**What you enter.** Everything you put into the app: tasks, habits and check-ins, study sessions, job applications and interviews, workouts and body measurements, sleep, water, steps and weight, transactions, budgets and savings goals, notes and journal entries, goals, reminders and focus sessions. Some of this (health, fitness and financial information) can be sensitive. You decide what to record; none of it is required.

**Settings.** Your time zone, currency, theme, targets and notification preferences.

**Notifications.** If you turn on push notifications on a phone, we store a device push token, the platform (iOS or Android) and an optional device name so we can send your own reminders to that device. We remove the token when you sign out of that device or turn the setting off.

**Technical data.** Our hosting and authentication providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.

## What we do not do
We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not use your data to train AI models, and the app has no AI assistant features.

## How we use it
To provide the app and sync it across your devices; to send the reminders and notifications you set up; to secure accounts and prevent abuse; and to respond to your support requests. Our legal basis (where such a concept applies) is performing our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running a secure service, and your consent for push notifications.

## Who processes it
- **Supabase** (database, authentication, realtime and server functions) stores your account and app data. Region: [region].
- **Expo** (push notification delivery) receives your push token and the notification text when a reminder is sent. Apple and Google deliver the notification to your device.
- **[Hosting provider]** serves the website.
These providers act on our instructions and have their own security commitments. Your data is protected in the database by row-level security so that only you can read or change your records.

## Where it is stored and for how long
We keep your data while your account exists. Data is held in Supabase's infrastructure in [region]; transfers across borders may occur as our providers operate internationally. Backups held by our provider are deleted on its normal schedule after you delete your account.

## Your choices and rights
- **Access and export:** the web app (Export page) lets you download a JSON backup and CSV files of your data.
- **Correct or delete records:** edit or delete anything in the app at any time.
- **Delete your account:** *Settings > Delete account* in the app (web and mobile). This permanently deletes your account and all of your data. It cannot be undone.
- **Notifications:** turn push off in the app's Notifications settings or in your phone's system settings.
- You may also have rights under local law (for example access, correction, erasure, portability, withdrawal of consent, or complaint to a regulator, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR). Contact us at [support email] and we will respond within [30] days.

## Children
LifeTrack is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit; sign-in sessions on phones are kept in the device's secure storage; every database table is protected by row-level security; our server credentials are never shipped inside the apps. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password.

If we make material changes we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app or by email.
