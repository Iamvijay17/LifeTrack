# Changelog

All notable changes to LifeTrack. Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` (`apps/mobile/app.config.ts` `version`, mirrored in `apps/web/package.json`);
store build numbers are auto-incremented by EAS. Add new entries under **Unreleased** in every pull request.

## [Unreleased]
See `docs/UX_AUDIT.md` for the full audit behind these changes, and `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` for the design and motion system.
### Changed
- **Settings → Account** no longer has its own change-password form (it did not check the current password); it links to Security & Privacy. The in-browser DEV backend now stores passwords as salted PBKDF2 instead of an unsalted SHA-256 (older dev accounts are upgraded when they sign in) and locks an account after repeated wrong passwords. DEV mode only; production builds never contained it.
- Settings (web): the page is regrouped into five labelled sections (Profile & appearance, Region & daily goals, Scoring & focus, Notifications & data, Account & security), with the group label in a left rail on wide screens and above the cards on phones. Preferences fields are grouped (region & calendar; daily goals & budget), and Notifications gets its own card beside Your data. No setting was added or removed.
- **A full visual redesign, on web and mobile.** Dark-first, with violet and indigo accents, new surfaces and elevation, and rounder corners. Every module now has its own colour and character: the whole app, including charts and progress, takes on the colour of the module you're in. No feature, API or data changes.
- Motion throughout, using Motion on the web and React Native's own Animated on mobile (no new native module): page transitions, a sliding navigation indicator, staggered content, numbers that count up, rings and bars that fill, animated dialogs, tabs, checkboxes and switches, and bottom sheets you can drag to dismiss. Everything respects the reduced-motion setting.
- Charts: gradient fills, softer grids, a shared 650 ms rhythm instead of the 1.5 s default.
- Calendar alerts are announced by the notification centre (a toast, or a browser notification when you are in another window) instead of by the calendar itself, so quiet hours and priority apply to them too. Calendar's own browser-notification switch keeps working for events.
- The notification page's switches are now **categories** (Reminders, Tasks, Calendar, Habits, Fitness, Finance, Learning, Jobs, Goals); one switch governs both reminders and the nudges of that module. "Clear read" became "Dismiss read" (moves to History instead of deleting).
### Added
- **Security & Privacy** (web, `/security`, plus a lock screen): an **app lock** with a 4 or 6 digit PIN (easy PINs refused, a progressive lockout that ends the session from the tenth wrong try), auto-lock when you leave or after 1 to 60 minutes idle, lock again when you return, **Lock now**, and a dark, animated PIN keypad. The lock is **enforced by the database**, per session: while it is locked, Finance, Journal and Notes refuse that device on the server too, not just on screen. **Fingerprint / face / device-PIN unlock** through WebAuthn passkeys (Touch ID, Windows Hello, Android biometrics; the PIN is always the fallback; LifeTrack never sees a fingerprint), with devices you can add, rename and remove. **Change password** now asks for the current one, shows a live strength meter, refuses common and easy-to-guess passwords, and signs your other devices out. **Where you're signed in**: see every session, sign out one, the others or all, and mark a phone *trusted*. New passwords at sign-up, reset and change must pass the strength rules; signing in is never checked, so no existing account is affected. **Run `0012_security.sql` before releasing** (additive, safe to re-run; until then the app opens as before). Fingerprint unlock also needs `supabase functions deploy webauthn` and two secrets; per-account password lockout needs a dashboard hook; both are described, with what is and is not protected, in `docs/APP_SECURITY.md`. **The mobile app has no lock screen yet**: with the lock on, mark the phone as trusted or Finance, Journal and Notes stay closed there.
- **Notification & Reminder Center** (web, `/notifications` and the bell): one inbox for your reminders, calendar alerts and new summaries from tasks, habits, fitness, finance, jobs, learning and goals ("3 tasks due today", "2 habits left", "Rent is due today", "Interview in 1 hour", budget warnings), made once however many devices are open. An animated drawer opens from the bell (a bottom sheet on a phone). **Snooze** (10 minutes to next week, or a date and time you pick), **dismiss** (kept in a searchable **History**, with Undo), **reschedule** a reminder, mark read and unread, swipe to dismiss on a phone. **Priorities** (low, normal, high, urgent), a note on reminders, and **weekdays** and **yearly** repeats. Search, category, priority and status filters. **Quiet hours** (urgent can break through; what was held is summed up afterwards), **desktop notifications** (permission asked only when you turn them on) and the times of the daily summaries in Preferences. A reminder that fired late is shown as **Missed · was due …** and an overdue one is flagged in Reminders. The send-reminders Edge Function copies priority, note and due time and does not push during quiet hours (an urgent reminder still does). The mobile app is unchanged apart from carrying the new columns. **Run `0013_notification_center.sql` before releasing** (additive, safe to re-run). See `docs/NOTIFICATIONS.md`. Smart notifications and calendar alerts need an open app; desktop notifications need an open tab.
- **Calendar & Timeline** (web, `/calendar`): Day, Week, Month and Agenda views over your tasks, events, interviews, reminders and deadlines, in your timezone. Click or drag out a slot to add an event or a task; drag to move, drag an edge to resize, drag a task from Unscheduled onto a day or time. Every change is saved straight away (shown at once, undone with the reason if the save fails), with mouse, touch (press and hold) or Alt + arrow keys. Events repeat daily, weekly, monthly, yearly or on a custom schedule, and changing one asks for this event, this and following, or all events. Alerts before an event or task become notifications (once, however many devices are open) and, if you turn it on, browser notifications. Search, category / priority / status filters, jump to a date, 12 / 24-hour time and shortcuts (`?`). Tasks made here are Planner tasks (no copies). Tasks now accept an optional time of day, `urgent` priority, the `Routine` and `Custom` categories and a yearly repeat. See `docs/CALENDAR.md`. **Run `0011_calendar.sql` before releasing** (additive, safe to re-run). Alerts do not reach a closed app (no server job for events yet).
- Food & Nutrition (web and mobile): log meals under Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks and Dinner with a calorie ring, protein, carbs, fat and fibre, a water tracker with undo, optional daily targets (nothing is assumed for you), a calendar that flags days with nothing logged, and weekly and monthly analytics. **Run migrations `0009_nutrition.sql` and `0010_food_database.sql` before releasing** (both additive and safe to re-run).
- Food database: about 290 built-in Indian foods, with extra depth in Tamil Nadu and South Indian food (idli and dosa varieties, pongal, parotta and kothu parotta, appam, idiyappam, puttu, meals, rice dishes, kuzhambu and poriyal, biryani, fried rice and noodles, chicken and fish starters, street food, North Indian curries and breads, drinks, sweets, fruit, vegetables, dairy and protein foods) in 14 categories. Every food has calories, protein, carbs, fat and fibre per serving, household serving sizes (piece, cup, plate, bowl, glass), an automatic 100 g (or 100 ml) size and a custom weight in grams, with the nutrition recalculating as you change the amount. Values are approximate and each is marked as a typical value, an approximate recipe, or a rough restaurant estimate. Search understands other spellings (dosai, thosai, briyani, kozhi kulambu), Tamil names (தோசை, பிரியாணி) and partly typed words, and you can add other names to your own foods. Adding a food that is already in the list is refused with a pointer to the existing one. Your own foods and edited copies of built-in ones are never touched when the built-in list grows.
- Dashboard, **Life Command Center** (web): five key numbers under the greeting (Life Score, today's completion, tasks today, overdue, upcoming events); new **Career & learning**, **Job switch**, **Gym & fitness**, **Sleep & health** and **Habits & routine** widgets; the Spending widget became **Finance this month** (income, expenses, budget left, recent transactions); the daily score became the **Life Score** (Today, 7 or 30 days, with points earned per category and the change on the previous period) with **trends** by day, week and month; Recent activity now includes sleep and shows each entry's module. Every figure is calculated from your own records and goals, and a day, category or period with nothing recorded is left out instead of counted as zero. Customize can now resize each widget (Small / Medium / Large / Full width), move widgets with arrows as well as by dragging, and **Restore default layout** (with Undo). Layout changes show at once and are saved to your account as one write, so they follow you to every device. No migration: the layout lives in the existing `user_settings.dashboard_widgets` column (`sizes` joins `order`), and the mobile app ignores what it does not know.
- Dashboard: a **Food today** widget (calories against your target, protein, carbs and fat) on web and mobile. It joins the widget list, so it can be hidden or moved in Customize. No migration.
- Log workout and routines (web and mobile): the exercise picker now shows each exercise's photo next to its name, your own exercises first and then matches from the exercise database. You can still type any name.
- Fitness → Exercises: an exercise database of 876 exercises from [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) (Unlicense, public domain) with start and end demonstration photos, instructions, muscles and equipment. Search, filter by Chest / Back / Shoulders / Biceps / Triceps / Legs / Abs / Cardio, equipment, level and type, open an exercise's own page, and add it to your exercises. Photos are hosted by LifeTrack, shown unaltered in one consistent frame, with a placeholder when none exists; your own exercises link to their demonstration. Mobile has the same database (search, categories, equipment filter, photos, add to your exercises), loading the catalog and each photo from the web app at `EXPO_PUBLIC_WEB_URL`. The photos (about 99 MB) are committed under `apps/web/public/exercises`; `exercises:check` verifies them against SHA-256 hashes and `exercises:sync` repairs any that are missing.
- Dashboard: a hero with a live clock, a 12-column layout, small seven-day charts on the tiles, and widgets you can drag into your own order (saved to your account; arrows on mobile).
- Command palette (Ctrl/Cmd + K): the search box now also runs quick actions, jumps to any page and switches the theme.
- Celebrations for real achievements: finishing the day's habits or the whole agenda, streak milestones, completing a goal or a focus block, reaching a savings target, landing an offer.
- Habits and Planner: a week strip to see and jump between days. Habits show the last seven days on each row.
- Job Switch: a pipeline bar, cards that glide between board columns. Fitness: a week of day tiles. Health: a night-sky sleep card and a water glass that fills. Finance: a ledger hero, a budget gauge and transactions grouped by day.
- Dashboard: **Today**, one ordered list of the whole day (interviews and reminders by time, then tasks, habits, the planned workout, revisions, follow-ups and bills due, finished items below). Tasks and habits can be ticked from it. Web and mobile; no migration.
- Dashboard: Upcoming deadlines (interviews, follow-ups, goals, milestones, reminders, overdue tasks), a getting-started guide for new accounts, stat cards that open their module, and Undo after completing a task.
- Fitness: personal records, per-exercise progress chart, month calendar in History.
- Health: a sleep goal in Settings (used by the score and the charts), and sleep regularity and energy averages. The goal is stored in the existing `habit_prefs` settings column; no migration.
- Finance: budget alert at 80% and when over budget, Duplicate on a transaction, and filters collapsed behind a button on phones.
- Finance: recurring rules for rent, EMI and subscriptions (web and mobile). Daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, with pause, resume and an end date; shown in the dashboard's Upcoming. Open apps post what is due, dated the day it fell due, once across devices. **Needs migration `0008_recurring_rules.sql` applied before release.**
- Planner: "Move to today" for overdue tasks; overdue tasks shown first on phones. Tap the date to jump with a calendar (Planner, Habits, Notes, Health).
- Learning: revision dates are picked from a calendar instead of typed.
- Global search opens the application, day or month you picked. Each page has its own browser title.
### Fixed
- Data export: CSV now covers the Food & Nutrition tables, learning topics, your exercises, routines and notifications, and the amount column is named after your currency instead of always INR.
- Backup restore: restoring into an account that already has nutrition targets or its own copy of a starter food no longer stops with "That record already exists"; food log entries follow the existing copy.
- Daily score: Personal, Finance, Gym and Health tasks now count (in Habits, Fitness and Sleep & Health); before, only Learning and Job Search tasks did. The default 120-minute study target applies only once you have studied in the last 14 days, so someone who doesn't study is no longer scored zero every day (the Today's progress list follows the same rule).
- Savings goals on web: adding money now happens inside the database, like mobile, so two devices can't overwrite each other's deposit.
- A long title made Dashboard and Planner wider than the screen on phones and tablets; the Fitness plan overflowed on phones.
- Calendars and menus were cut off inside dialogs and tables. Popups now draw above their container.
- Escape on an open dropdown or calendar closed the whole form and lost what was typed.
- The dashboard ignored a month's own budget and could disagree with Finance (web and mobile).
- Custom finance categories only existed in one browser; they now come from your transactions, so every device has them.
- Daily score and health chart used different sleep targets (7.5 h and 8 h).
- Purple focus line around the page after navigating; dialogs jumping while opening.
### Accessibility
- Colour contrast now meets WCAG AA in both themes (design tokens changed on web and mobile; new `primarySolid` and `dangerSolid`).
- Keyboard support in menus and tabs; job table rows reachable by keyboard; labelled heatmap; correct heading order; invalid fields marked; hints announced; chart summaries; 40–44px touch targets on phones; global reduced-motion support.
### Changed
- Typing into a form and dismissing it by accident (Esc, X, clicking outside) now asks before discarding.
- New accounts no longer seed a topic named after a personal project (existing accounts are unchanged).

## [1.0.0] - first release (release candidate)
### Added
- Web app (PWA) with 12 modules: planner, learning, jobs, fitness, health, finance, habits, focus, notes, goals, analytics, notifications.
- iOS and Android app (Expo) sharing the same Supabase backend and the same domain logic as the web app.
- Push reminders (Edge Function + Expo push), per-device opt-in, wall-clock reminders in the user's time zone.
- In-app account deletion; JSON/CSV export on web; public privacy policy and terms pages.
### Security
- Row Level Security on every user-owned table with an automated isolation suite; cross-table ownership triggers (migrations 0004/0005).
- Atomic water and savings counters (`add_water` in 0004, `add_savings` in 0007).
### Known limitations
- Online-first (no offline editing); Reports, Export/Import and global search are web-only.
